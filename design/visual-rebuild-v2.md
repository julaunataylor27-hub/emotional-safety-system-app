# Emotional Safety System — visual rebuild (screen by screen)

**Reference:** User-supplied ten-screen design image (9 October 2026).
**Working branch:** `design-reference-one-screen-at-a-time`.
**Status:** Visual direction approved as reference; individual Home graphic pending review.

## Design direction
- Rich midnight forest / almost black backgrounds, warm glowing gold frames and buttons.
- Detailed golden tree of life with illuminated roots as Home's central emblem.
- Sunset Country landscape with ochre, copper, amber and deep green, not a plain flat design.
- Clear white/cream text, gold headlines; readable on an Android-sized phone.
- Small colourful icon illustrations for Knowledge, Understanding, Love, Kindness, Patience, Growth, Humanity and Life.
- Distinct, tappable components (no text baked into a single static screenshot).
- Aboriginal-inspired motifs require careful, appropriate art direction; avoid generic claims that a motif is a specific cultural design.
