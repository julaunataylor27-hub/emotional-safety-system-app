# Two Flames — Healing Without Control
**Date:** 24 October 2026
**Category:** Emotional Safety System / learning concepts
**Status:** Prototype learning activity (not clinically validated)

## Purpose
Introduce a positive, inclusive learning metaphor within the app: two individuals can encourage each other without becoming responsible for the other person’s healing or controlling their choices.

> Two flames can share their warmth without one burning itself out for the other.

## Learning flow
1. **My flame** — maintain personal choice, identity, boundaries and pace.
2. **Their flame** — support another person's autonomy without assuming their feelings or forcing disclosure.
3. **Shared light** — freely chosen kindness, laughter, care and mutual encouragement.

Four optional tap-to-explore prompts cover joy, space, choice/consent and honest communication. Selections are not stored or sent anywhere. The screen links to existing support resources.

## Safety and scope
- Do not assess, diagnose, rank or certify any relationship as safe.
- Do not imply that one person is responsible for healing another.
- Either adult may decline closeness, change their mind or end an arrangement.
- The content is educational reflection rather than therapy, legal advice or an agreement.
- Avoid references to identifiable individuals, private stories or personal details.

## Navigation and presentation
- Reachable from Home, Learning Centre and More.
- Night blue, gold and warm flame motifs match the existing main-app palette.
- No new external library, internet requirement, data collection, account or permission.
