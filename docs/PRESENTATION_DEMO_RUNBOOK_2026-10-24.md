# Australian Humanity — prototype presentation runbook

**Prepared:** 24 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Reliable, professional demonstration for an upcoming meeting.  
**Status:** Draft demonstration guide, not proof of scientific or government endorsement.

## Opening (around one minute)
"Australian Humanity is a working educational prototype of my Emotional Safety System. The idea came from lived experience and the question: what if we could teach people to recognise coercive patterns and strengthen personal choice before harm escalates?"

## Recommended live walkthrough (about 6–8 minutes)
1. **Home → Australian Humanity — Presentation Overview.** Explain "Knowledge Creates Choice" and the reason for building it.
2. **Purpose and process.** Advance through slides 1 and 2: feeling, story, six signals, truth check, cultural reflection and a safe next step.
3. **What has been built.** Point to Journey, Knowledge, Evidence & Truth, Two Flames and Support. Be clear that proposed AI/EI intelligence is a future development direction.
4. **Live demonstration.** In overview step 4, tap **Explore a situation** and use a fictional adult scenario: "A person says they need an evening alone. Someone keeps sending messages and threatens to withdraw support unless they reply." Show the *prototype's* interpretation, not a diagnosis or determination of harm.
5. **Evidence & Truth.** Show how a record distinguishes a reported event from a conclusion. Describe uncertainty, gaps and a choice to seek further reliable sources.
6. **Culture & learning.** Explain how Country, kinship, history and belonging can inform respectful inquiry. Emphasise that Aboriginal Nations are distinct and content requires community permission and guidance.
7. **Support.** Show that users can find support without completing any reflection or test.
8. **Return to overview.** Use slides 5–6 to discuss safeguards, independent evaluation, consultation, accessibility, security and partnerships.

## Backup route for the day
- Open the already successful Android release on your own Samsung phone before the meeting.
- Charge the phone; consider screen mirroring and keep the APK file available. Test connectivity and screen sharing beforehand.
- Use the six-stage document here as a paper or saved-text backup if the app cannot be shown.
- Do not rely on an unfinished build or on a presentation feature that has not passed the final installed Android smoke test.
- Do not expose private journals, identifiable family history or someone else's circumstances while presenting. Clear optional example fields before demonstration if necessary.

## Grounded claims
**Demonstrable:** Buttons, local learning pages, pathway concepts, review screens, support links, selected device-local notes and PDF controls (when tested on the phone).  
**Proposed:** AI/EI interpretation beyond current code, phone-level filtering chips, clinical effectiveness, official cultural endorsement, universal coverage, future partnerships.  
**Never claim:** The prototype proves abuse, consent, guilt, people's inner feelings, identity or legal compliance.

## Presentation quality checklist
- Portrait Android screen readable at native size
- Matching header, backgrounds, navigation and card treatment
- Support visible and reachable quickly
- Text readable over images; sufficient touch targets
- Device back navigation works
- No demo stalls, blank screens or failing links
- Notes and PDFs operate as previously tested
- All headings spelled correctly; six core signals consistent
- No private information visible
- Full Android build, unit tests and emulator smoke test passed on **the same exact commit**
