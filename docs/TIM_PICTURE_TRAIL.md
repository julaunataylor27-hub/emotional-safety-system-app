# Tim Berners-Lee picture and decision trail · 1.7.8

Open **Learn → Knowledge Trail → Explore Tim’s story**. The approved picture
history contains twelve milestones from the March 1989 proposal to the memoir
first published in 2025 and its 2026 paperback edition. A milestone selector and
Previous/Next controls allow chronological or direct exploration. Pictures are
displayed normally, without tap-to-enlarge, zoom, panning or a lightbox.

Each milestone includes an image caption, image provenance and reuse terms,
a historical record link, and the decision trail: our question, the recorded
action, what the evidence establishes, and our next connection. The learning
framework and prompts are explicitly app-created. They do not reconstruct
private thoughts or establish a complete algorithm in Berners-Lee’s mind.
Later museum photographs, later software screenshots and reconstructions are
dated separately from the original milestone. Earlier Oxford and ENQUIRE work
is linked as background, with the absence of verified photographs stated.

The existing five-step learning example remains available separately. Personal
questions, optional sources, uncertainty, session notes and sharing keep their
existing behaviour. Entering the picture history does not send personal writing
to a source, image server, browser or AI service.

Five freely licensed/public-domain pictures are bundled: the existing Paul
Clarke portrait, Coolcaesar’s first-server photograph, the archived browser
screenshot, the 2021 browser-emulator reconstruction, and Maximilien Brice’s
2009 reunion photograph. See [image credits](../assets/learning/README.md).

Six CERN archive images and the official book-cover image are displayed from
their original HTTPS locations for this educational view. They are not copied
into the public repository or redistributed as reusable assets. Opening a
milestone with an archive image makes an image request to that host; no notes
are attached. The interface labels the internet requirement, retains captions
and evidence when an image is unavailable, and offers image retry and the
original source. External source and licence links require a tap and include
manual addresses and retry after failure. Inclusion implies no endorsement.

Version 1.7.8 uses Android versionCode 26 and the existing app package. The
release workflow still applies the existing source patches; generated App.js
must not be committed. Component checks cover all milestones, provenance,
missing-image recovery, source failures, Android Back, decision visibility,
personal-note preservation and the absence of image zoom. Installed Android
testing checks the proposal, decision labels and the bundled first-server
picture before testing the existing five-step example and personal trail.
