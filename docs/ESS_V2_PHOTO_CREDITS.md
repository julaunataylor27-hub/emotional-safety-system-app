# ESS v2 — 2027 photography credits and asset handling

All photography is credited to its creator under the applicable Creative Commons license. Downloading/resizing for the Android APK does not imply a claim that any photographed place or cultural imagery represents a particular Aboriginal Nation, sacred place or community-approved narrative. Landscape photos are atmospheric and informational, not proof of a historical event or Indigenous authorship.

Images are bundled locally in the compiled APK by `v2/scripts/prepare-scenes.mjs` (1400px maximum width). The original photographs remain accessible at the links below; actual presentation is center-cropped / shaded by native UI. If transformed/cropped, derivative image material remains subject to its original license including share-alike conditions where applicable. Attribution does not imply endorsement.
