# Home card artwork refresh — 21 October 2026

User-approved visual direction: black and forest-green UI, warm gold borders and four **different** scenic photographs/illustrations on Home.

- Knowledge: illuminated new growth over mountain country (`knowledge-growth-v1.webp`)
- Emotional Safety: a seated person reflecting in a golden sunset (`emotional-sunset-v1.webp`)
- Evidence & Truth: research and record books in a warmly lit archive (`evidence-records-v1.webp`)
- Support & Services: a small group together at a landscape lookout (`support-community-v1.webp`)

The four WebP images are cropped and adapted from the approved AI-generated home mock-up of this date, and are separate offline assets. They are **symbolic visual illustrations**, not historical photographs, evidence of events, or culturally endorsed representations. Artwork is only a visual change: the established native routes, visible text, accessibility labels, support action, extra values and learning functions remain interactive. Do not replace the Home screen with a screenshot.

Validation: `npm test` and Android APK build/smoke checks should pass before announcing a working installed update. Reinstalling a new APK is necessary to see a source-code change on a standalone Android installation.
